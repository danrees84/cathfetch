> As he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and unspotted in his sight in charity.

> A voice in Rama was heard, lamentation and great mourning; Rachel bewailing her children, and would not be comforted, because they are not.

Matthew gives the bereaved mothers of Bethlehem the voice of Rachel's scriptural lament. > And he said to them: How is it that you sought me? did you not know, that I must be about my father’s business?

The young Jesus' answer reveals his unique relation to the Father within the life of his human family. Mary and Joseph do not immediately understand, and Luke preserves their perplexity. > After that, he saith to the disciple: Behold thy mother. And from that hour, the disciple took her to his own.

**Psalms 23:2** — Douay–Rheims (1899)

> For he hath founded it upon the seas; and hath prepared it upon the rivers.

The psalm grounds the inhabited world's belonging to the Lord in his creative act. Human life stands upon a gift it did not establish for itself. The later question about entering God's holy place therefore concerns truthful and innocent conduct before the Creator of all. The response holds the worship of the sanctuary together with responsibility within God's world.
