**Ephesians 1:4** — Douay–Rheims (1899)

> As he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and unspotted in his sight in charity.

The letter locates election in Christ before any human achievement. Its purpose is holiness in love, not a superiority that believers can claim over others. God's initiative opens a life of communion and praise in which the gifts of redemption are received with thanksgiving. The Church hears this blessing as thanksgiving for the grace of belonging to Christ.

Category: **second** · Testament: **new** · Appointed reading: Eph 1:3-6, 15-18
