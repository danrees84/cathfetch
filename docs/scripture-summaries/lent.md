**Matthew 18:22** — Douay–Rheims (1899)

> Jesus saith to him: I say not to thee, till seven times; but till seventy times seven times.

Peter asks about the limits of forgiveness, and Jesus answers by overturning the arithmetic of resentment. The following parable locates this demand within the mercy already received from God. Forgiveness is not denial of injury, but refusal to make another's debt the final truth of the relationship. The repeated weekday proclamation returns forgiveness to the centre of life within the community.
